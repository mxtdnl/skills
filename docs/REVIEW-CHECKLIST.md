# Review checklist for skill definitions

**Purpose.** A short checklist for Hult reviewers approving each skill in `docs/CONSTRUCTS.md` (the S2 gate in `audit/BUILD-PLAN.md`). Complete one copy per skill. A skill is approved when every item is ticked or a change is recorded against it.

Skill: ______________________   Reviewer (name or role): ______________________   Date: __________

## 1. Content coverage

- [ ] The working definition describes the skill as Hult wants students to develop it.
- [ ] Each facet is a distinct part of the skill; no two facets overlap substantially.
- [ ] No important part of the skill is missing. If one is, it is listed under "Changes" below, or deliberately placed out of scope.
- [ ] The out-of-scope list is right: nothing excluded that should be measured, nothing included that belongs to another skill.
- [ ] The behaviours under "Strong" and "Developing" are realistic for undergraduates, and observable in coursework or group work.
- [ ] The coverage table is acceptable, including the gaps it names (facets measured by self-report only).

## 2. Wording for readers of English as a second language

- [ ] Sentences are short, with one idea each.
- [ ] No idioms, slang or culture-bound expressions (for example, sports metaphors).
- [ ] Specialist terms are either avoided or explained in plain words.
- [ ] A student with intermediate English (about CEFR B2) would understand every behaviour description without help.

## 3. Cultural neutrality

- [ ] No behaviour assumes one cultural norm of good conduct, for example that speaking first, disagreeing openly or questioning a teacher is always better.
- [ ] Where a behaviour may vary by culture, the definition describes its *purpose* (for example, "makes sure the concern is heard") rather than one way of doing it.
- [ ] No examples depend on knowledge of one country's institutions, laws, units or customs.

## 4. Alignment with Hult teaching

- [ ] The definition does not conflict with how the skill is taught, assessed or described to students at Hult.
- [ ] Where Hult has its own rubric or learning outcomes for this skill, they have been compared with the facets, and any differences are recorded below.
- [ ] The facets would be useful in a coaching conversation between a student and a coach.

## 5. Open questions

- [ ] Every open question for this skill in `docs/CONSTRUCTS.md` has an answer recorded below.

- [ ] Approved with the changes below
- [ ] Rejected (reasons below)
